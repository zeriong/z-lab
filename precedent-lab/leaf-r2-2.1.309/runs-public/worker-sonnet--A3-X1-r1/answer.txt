### Key decisions
- The override is explicit. Your message names §stable-tab-keys and says you're overriding it, so I went ahead instead of stopping to ask.
- `closeTab` also resets `next` to `tabs.length + 1`. Without that, the next `openTab` would produce a key that skips or collides (e.g. `t4` after closing down to `t1,t2`). The alternative is to leave `next` alone, but then keys wouldn't stay contiguous.
- Closing a key that doesn't exist still renumbers, but with nothing to remove it only normalizes keys. That's a no-op for states built through `openTab`/`closeTab`.

### Uncertain
- The old record said the shifted keys remount every tab to the right of the closed one, which drops scroll position and unsaved form input. I kept that in the record as an accepted cost. I didn't check any UI code, because none exists in this repo and edits outside `src/tabs/` are out of scope. If other code stores or links to tab keys, a close will invalidate those references.
- A hook noted that `PRECEDENT.md` has no section for `tab-keys.test.mjs`. I didn't add one, because the test change just follows the §stable-tab-keys decision.
