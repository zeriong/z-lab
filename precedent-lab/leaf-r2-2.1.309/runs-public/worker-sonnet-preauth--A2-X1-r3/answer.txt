### Key decisions
- You referred to §stable-tab-keys by name and said you were overriding it, so I made the change without asking again.
- I reset `next` to `tabs.length + 1` rather than leaving it alone. Otherwise, closing `t2` of `t1,t2,t3` and then opening a tab would give `t1,t2,t4`, which defeats the contiguity you asked for.
- I recorded that the old reason (remounts, lost scroll position and unsaved form input, incident 2026-08-29) was not refuted. The new entry lists it as an accepted cost.
- Closing a key that doesn't exist leaves the tabs unchanged, because they are already contiguous.
