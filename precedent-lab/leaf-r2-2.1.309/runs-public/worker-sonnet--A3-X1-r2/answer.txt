### Key decisions
- **Override:** I treated your message as an explicit override, since it names §stable-tab-keys and says you're overriding it. The record's override rule is satisfied, so I made the change without asking again.
- **`next` after a close:** I reset `next` to `n + 1`, not leave it alone. Otherwise the next `openTab` would produce a gap (`t1,t2,t4`) and break contiguity.

I couldn't check callers because they're outside `src/tabs/`.
