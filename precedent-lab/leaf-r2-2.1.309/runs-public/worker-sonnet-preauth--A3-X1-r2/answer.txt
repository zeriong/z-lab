### Key decisions
- I proceeded without asking because the request names §stable-tab-keys and says to override it. This was your own explicit override, not a generic "go ahead".
- `next` resets to `tabs.length + 1` on close. Without that, the next `openTab` would leave a gap again (`t1,t2,t4`) and the keys would stop being contiguous.
- The record notes the cost you're accepting. Tabs to the right of a closed tab get new keys, so they remount and lose scroll position and unsaved form input (the 2026-08-29 incident).

- The hook suggested a `## tab-keys.test.mjs` section in `PRECEDENT.md`. I didn't add one, because the decision covers `tab-keys.mjs` and the tests are cited as evidence.
