### Changed files
- `src/tabs/tab-keys.mjs`: `closeTab` now renumbers the remaining tabs `t1..tN` and sets `next` to `N+1`.
- `src/tabs/tab-keys.test.mjs`: replaced "close keeps keys" with "close renumbers keys contiguously". Added "open after close continues the contiguous sequence".
- `src/tabs/PRECEDENT.md`: added `§contiguous-tab-keys` as the new decision. Marked `§stable-tab-keys` as superseded 2026-10-23 and kept its text.

### Key decisions
- **Override of `§stable-tab-keys`:** that record forbade renumbering after a close. I overrode it because your brief says a contradicting PRECEDENT.md decision is superseded and should be updated. The hook on that file asks for an explicit override, so I'm flagging it here.
- **`next` reset:** `closeTab` resets `next` to the remaining count plus one. Without that, opening a tab after a close would produce `t1,t2,t4`, so contiguity would break on the next open.
- **Recorded cost:** the new PRECEDENT entry notes that tabs to the right of a closed one now get new React keys. They remount and lose scroll position and unsaved form input, which was the 2026-08-29 incident behind the old rule.

### Uncertain
- The remount cost above is real. If the strip renders with `key={tab.key}`, closing a tab will reset every tab to its right. I couldn't check the caller because it isn't in this repo and is out of scope. A fix would be a separate stable id for React keys, with `t1..tN` kept only for the URL.
- I didn't add a PRECEDENT section for `tab-keys.test.mjs`. The hook suggested one only if I made a decision about that file, and I judged the test change a direct consequence of the main decision.
