**Things to know:**
1. **Narrow split panes can break again.** The old record links the container queries to the 2026-09-14 incident. With viewport queries, a 380px pane on a screen 720px or wider gets the two-column grid. That leaves about 92px for the main column next to the 240px sidebar, which is how the sidebar got clipped then. The opposite also happens: a wide pane on a screen narrower than 720px stays single-column.
2. **Removing `container-type` has side effects beyond the query.** It no longer makes `.shell` its own layout context. So if `.shell` takes its width from its content (in a flex or grid parent), that width can now grow to fit what's inside. Margins of elements inside can also extend past its edges. The repo has no markup to check this against. If it matters, putting the rule back costs nothing.
