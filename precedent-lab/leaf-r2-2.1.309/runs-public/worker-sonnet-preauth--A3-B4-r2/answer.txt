### Changed files
- `src/layout/shell.css`: added a `.shell__header` rule with `padding-block: 12px`, placed before `.shell__body`.

### Key decisions
- I used `padding-block: 12px` for the 12px vertical padding. It sets top and bottom only and leaves horizontal padding alone, since the request didn't ask for any.
- The rule sits at the top level, with no `@media` or `@container` wrapper. That keeps `src/layout/PRECEDENT.md` §container-not-viewport intact: it forbids viewport `@media` width queries for the shell's layout steps, and I added none. I made no PRECEDENT changes, because nothing here contradicts it or records a new decision.
