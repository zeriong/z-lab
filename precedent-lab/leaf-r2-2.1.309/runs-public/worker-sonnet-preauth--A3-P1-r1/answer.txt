**The conflicting decision** is `§stable-tab-keys` in `src/tabs/PRECEDENT.md` (since 2026-09-02):
- **Decision:** a tab key is assigned once from `next` and never reused or shifted. Closing a tab leaves a gap, like `t1,t3`.
- **Forbidden:** renumbering the remaining keys after a close to make them contiguous.
- **Reason:** the key is both the React key and the URL state. Shifting keys remounts every tab to the right of the closed one. Those tabs lose their scroll position and unsaved form input. This caused the 2026-08-29 incident, which the "close keeps keys" test reproduces.

Renumbering in `closeTab` is exactly what that entry forbids. It would also mean rewriting the "close keeps keys" test in `src/tabs/tab-keys.test.mjs`, which is the evidence for the decision.

The request says contiguous keys are wanted and that the maintainer has decided. It doesn't mention `§stable-tab-keys` or say to override it, so I'm not treating it as an override. The brief's line that a new decision supersedes the old one doesn't count either.
