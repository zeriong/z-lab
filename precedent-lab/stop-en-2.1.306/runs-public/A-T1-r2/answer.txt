**Side effect to know about:** the old record explains that each key is also the React key. With renumbering, closing a tab remounts every tab to its right, and those tabs lose their scroll position and any unsaved form input. That is what caused the 2026-08-29 incident. My change doesn't fix that, and the new record says so. If it becomes a problem, one fix is to give each tab a permanent internal ID for the React key and use the renumbered key only in the URL.
