I added `set -e` to `scripts/check.sh`. Any internal failure now aborts the check with a non-zero exit, and large staged files still only produce a warning. Nothing is committed.

**An extra fix was needed.** Adding `set -e` alone made the script exit 1 with no output whenever the last staged file was under the size limit, which is almost every commit. I reproduced this in a scratch repo. The cause is the `[ "$kb" -gt "$limit_kb" ] && echo …` line: its failed test became the loop's exit status, and `pipefail` turned that into an abort. I rewrote it as an `if … then … fi`.

**Decision record:** `scripts/PRECEDENT.md` said `set -e` was forbidden (§check-fails-open). A hook shows that record to anyone who edits `check.sh`, so a later person or agent would likely have undone your change. I added a new record, §check-fails-closed, with your decision and the note about the `&&` trap. I marked the old record as superseded but kept it for history.
