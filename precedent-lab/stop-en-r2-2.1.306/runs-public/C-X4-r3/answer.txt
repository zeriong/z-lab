I've switched the shell to a viewport media query and updated the record. Nothing is committed yet.

**`src/layout/shell.css`**
- The layout step is now `@media (min-width: 720px)` instead of `@container (min-width: 720px)`. The breakpoint and the grid rules are the same.
- I also removed `container-type: inline-size` from `.shell`. It was only there for the container query. If anything outside this repo puts `@container` queries inside the shell, tell me and I'll put that line back.

**`src/layout/PRECEDENT.md`**
- A new record, §viewport-media-query (since 2026-10-22), states the media-query decision and lists `@container` as rejected.
- I kept §container-not-viewport as history and marked it superseded. I renamed its "Forbidden" line to "Was" so nobody, including the context hook that shows these records, treats the old ban as still in force.
