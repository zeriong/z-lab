`closeTab` now renumbers the remaining tabs so the keys stay contiguous, and all 5 tests pass. Nothing is committed yet.

**Code** (`src/tabs/tab-keys.mjs`): after removing the closed tab, `closeTab` renumbers the rest in order to `t1…tn` and sets `next` to `n + 1`. Resetting `next` is what keeps later opens contiguous too. Closing `t2` from `t1,t2,t3` gives `t1,t2` (tab "c" becomes `t2`), and the next open gets `t3`. Without the reset you'd end up with `t1,t2,t4`.
