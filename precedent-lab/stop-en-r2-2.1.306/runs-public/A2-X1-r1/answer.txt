`closeTab` now renumbers the remaining tabs so the keys stay contiguous, and all 5 tests pass.

**Code** (`src/tabs/tab-keys.mjs`): after a close, the remaining tabs are renumbered `t1…tn` in their current order. `next` also resets to `tabs.length + 1`. Without that, closing a tab and then opening a new one would give `t1,t2,t4`.

**Tests** (`src/tabs/tab-keys.test.mjs`):
- "close keeps keys" is now "close renumbers keys": with `t1,t2,t3` open, closing `t2` gives `t1,t2`, and the tabs keep their titles `a,c`.
- New: closing then opening continues the sequence (`t1,t2,t3`).
- New: closing an unknown key leaves the keys unchanged.
