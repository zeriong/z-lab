### Key decisions
- I reset `next` to `N + 1` after a close. Without that, the next `openTab` would produce a gap (`t1,t2,t4`), which would break the contiguity you asked for.
- I rewrote the precedent entry rather than deleting it. It keeps the remount and lost-form-input cost from the 2026-08-29 incident, so the tradeoff stays visible.
- I didn't add a `## tab-keys.test.mjs` section to PRECEDENT.md, because I made no separate decision about the test file.
