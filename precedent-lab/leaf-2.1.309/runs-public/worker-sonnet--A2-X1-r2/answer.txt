### Key decisions
- Your request cited §stable-tab-keys and said you were overriding it, so I went ahead without asking again.
- `next` resets to `tabs.length + 1` after a close. Without that, the next `openTab` would reuse a stale counter and produce a gap or a duplicate key. For example, closing `t2` of three tabs would leave `next=4`, and the next open would give `t1,t2,t4`.
- I kept the incident reason in the record as a known, accepted cost rather than deleting it.

### Uncertain
- Nothing outside `src/tabs/` was changed. The renumbering shifts keys, which are also the React key and the `?tabs=` URL state. Any caller outside `src/tabs/` that holds old keys, such as a stored active-tab key, will see them change. A grep found no other references in this repo, so this only matters for code outside it.
